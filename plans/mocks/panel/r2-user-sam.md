# r2 panel · Sam (frontend dev, first day with Worca)

Gut reaction written from the images before I read r2-ux.md. I tried to ignore the grey captions in r2-ux.png, because the real app won't have them.

## 1. First 10 seconds

**Today.** The composer reads like a Mad Libs sentence: "Task on billing-api with Default on Opus 5.5 high from origin · latest then open a PR". I can tell it's settings for something, but it's a whole paragraph of settings before I've typed a word. I'd click "billing-api" first because it's the only word I recognise as mine. I can't tell what "Default", "high" or "origin · latest" mean. In the list, "Showing all from all projects by date ···" looks like a filter bar. The red ✕ on "Update Node to 24" made me think it had failed.

**r2.** "Describe the task…" with a send arrow. Fine, it looks like ChatGPT, so I know what to do. The line under it, "Task · billing-api · Opus 5.5, thorough · opens a PR", reads like a summary I can mostly understand. In the list, "+ New chat ⌘N" is the first thing I'd click. I still can't tell what "Task ⌄" changes into.

## 2. "Bump Node to 24 in billing-api and open a PR"

**Today.** I'd hunt for a text box first. Then I'd hesitate on every word in the sentence: is "Task" what I want, or something else? What's "Default"? Is "high" cost or quality? "origin · latest", is that a branch? I'd probably leave everything alone and hope "open a PR" does what it says. Words that mean nothing to me: Default, high, origin · latest, Customise. Also "Every task you start is in the list beside", which list?

**r2.** I'd type the sentence, see that billing-api and "opens a PR" are already set, and hit Enter. Then a "Proposed task" card with Start appears. That surprised me a bit, because I thought I'd already sent it, but it's reassuring that it asks first. Then Enter or click Start. I'd hesitate on "Task ⌄" (are there other kinds?) and "thorough" (slower? pricier?). "Push only" and "Leave it on a branch" are clear. "Workflow: Default · From: latest" in the menu is still jargon.

## 3. Clickable or just text

- Today: the bold words with ⌄ look clickable, and the grey connector words don't. That much works.
- r2: without the arrows I wouldn't know that "billing-api" or "Opus 5.5, thorough" are clickable until I hovered. Only "Task ⌄" looks like a control.
- **⌘N row:** a button to start a new chat, with its shortcut. Clear.
- **Title ⌄ ("Chat ⌄"):** I guessed it switches between apps or sections. I didn't guess filters.
- **Amber ?:** "something has a question for me" or "unknown status". Better than the red ✕, which I'd read as broken.
- **Dot after a value:** I'd guess "unsaved" or "live". "You changed this from the default" never crossed my mind. The md confirms that's what it means, so I'd need it explained once.

## 4. Starting a new chat

Today: the + at the top right, but I wasn't sure if that + makes a chat, a task or a project. r2: "+ New chat" at the top of the list. I found it immediately.

## 5. Which one I'd learn on

r2, easily. It starts with a text box like every chat tool I know, and the settings are short enough to skim.

What would make my first day easier:
1. **A one-line hint on hover for each setting**, in plain words (for example "thorough: thinks longer, slower"), and a clear way to tell what's clickable.
2. **Explain Task vs the other modes once**, when I first open that menu, so I don't have to guess what I'm choosing.
3. **Status icons that say what they mean** in words on hover ("Waiting for your answer"), and never red unless something actually broke.
