**Pass.** The composer is one line I can check and change before Enter, by keyboard. The proposal comes up at once with Start focused, Esc gives my text back, and remembered values show a dot with "Kept from your last worca-cc task". All three of my r3 fixes are in.

## The reversals

- **New chat back in the header, with a word:** fine. It's at the top and it says what it does, which is what I wanted.
- **⌥⌘N instead of ⌘N:** worse. A three-key chord for something I do 15 times a day. I get it if ⌘N belongs to the browser. Not a blocker, as long as it focuses the composer.
- **Light chevrons on each value:** fine, better even. Four faint ⌄ show me where my Tab stops are.
- **Spacing instead of "·":** fine. The dot now means one thing, changed, and that's worth it.
- **List scope in ⋯ at rest:** fine. Nothing above Needs you, good.
- **Narrowed list as the full 2-line sentence plus "Reset view":** worse. With 3 repos I narrow to one all day, and that's two lines plus a reset line on a 14" screen, just to say "needs you, billing-api". The title chip from r2 (`Needs you · billing-api ✕`) was one line.
- **Workflow and base in Customise:** fine. I touch them maybe once a week.
