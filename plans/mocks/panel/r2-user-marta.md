# Marta, engineering lead: round 2

## Where Dana changes my mind
I picked Choose after because one extra Start click costs me nothing at one task a day. Dana starts 15. Fifteen extra round trips is a real tax, and her point about a guessed repo is fair. If Worca guesses billing-api wrong and I click Start without reading, that's worse than seeing it before I send. So I'll give up the proposal step. What I actually liked about it was the **plain one-line summary I can check at a glance**, and a line that sits before Send can give me that too.

## Where her preference would hurt me
- **Keyboard cycling and per-repo memory** are fine as long as they stay invisible. If they turn into hints, key badges or extra marks on the line, my two-minute visit gets busier.
- **"Remember last choice"** worries me a little. If Dana picked "push only" yesterday on a shared setup, I don't want to inherit it silently. A remembered value must look like a *changed* one, never like a default.
- Every value as its own click target is fine, as long as it doesn't come back as seven chevrons.

## What I'd trade
I take the choice-before-send line, and in return:
1. It stays **one line, in words**.
2. "as usual" hides what I don't understand.
3. Changed values stand out.

## The designer's proposal
**Composer: `Task on billing-api, Opus 5.5 high, as usual`.** Yes, this fixes what bothered me. "with Default" and "origin · latest" are gone behind "as usual", which is the most human phrase anyone has put on this line. It's one line, and when something changed (`Opus 5.5 max, then push only`) I can read it. Does it still look like status text? A bit. At meta size in grey, the dotted underline is faint on a big dark monitor, and I'd likely never notice it on my phone. It works if hover fills it and the first click on any word opens something. "high" is still unclear to me. "Opus 5.5 · high effort" once, in the menu, would settle it.

**`+ New chat ⌘N` as the first list row.** This is exactly what I asked for: a word, where my eyes already are. Good.

**"Showing all by date ⋯" kept at rest.** This one I still don't want. It's shorter, but it's a line telling me nothing changed, every visit, right above Needs you. Show it only when I've narrowed the list, the way the "list narrowed" panel with Reset already does. At rest, put the ⋯ in the header.
