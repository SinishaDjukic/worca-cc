# Round 1 synthesis

Panel: Dana (power user, 10–20 tasks/day), Marta (lead, checks what needs her), UX designer. Worked independently.
Files: r1-user-dana.md, r1-user-marta.md, r1-ux.md / r1-ux.png.

## All three agree
- The header + is wrong: it reads as a window control or "add project", far from the rows, says nothing. New chat needs a word and/or ⌘N.
- Defaults that only say "as always" (with Default, origin · latest) should not take ink.
- Model and effort are one choice, one picker.
- Bold + chevron on every value is the main source of weight; connector words add width.
- The list sentence when nothing is filtered is noise.

## Disagreements to resolve
1. **Choose before or after.** Dana: before, in a fixed spot, every value clickable and keyboard-reachable; a proposal+Start per task is a step too many at 15/day. Marta: after (type → proposal → Start) feels natural. UX: before, quiet sentence on one meta line.
2. **Does the list keep a sentence at rest?** Both users: no. UX: one meta line "Showing all by date ⋯".
3. **Readout affordance.** Marta: grey values read as status, not as clickable. UX: dotted underline + hover fill.
4. **Changed values: violet or ink 500?** Earlier mocks used violet; UX says violet already means "Worca is writing".
5. **New chat.** UX: first list row "+ New chat ⌘N". Dana: ⌘N / from the composer. Marta: "+ New" with a word at the top of the list, or typing on home starts one.

## Smaller notes
- Marta: "high" is unclear (effort? priority?); red ✕ on a row is ambiguous between failed and needs you.
- Dana: remember last choice per repo; repo should be the brightest value.
