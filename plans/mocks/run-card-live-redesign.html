What changed and why

  1. Detail line names the step. "3rd pass" becomes "Manual web UI testing · Review, pass 3 · 12m in this step". The headline stays the state word, as Card head asks.
  2. Route folds into the meta line while it is all future. Two of its three rows said what has not happened yet. The full route comes back once a row has news (a PR, checks, a conflict).
  3. Three figures replace the two tiles. Time, cost and cost per hour, unboxed. The rate is what tells you whether $83 is a problem.
  4. Now and Next. The agent's latest action and what happens after this step. Nothing on today's card says either.
  5. Tabs replace Results and How it ran. Today they are boxed lists inside the card; as tabs each shows a count and opens in place, and Workflow gives the live graph a home.
  6. Facts move to Overview. Group and Labels show as quiet adds, not rows that read "None".

Rules this would change in docs/design-system.md

  1. Route: "at every level from the start of the run" becomes "once a row has a state other than future".
  2. Patterns › run and group page head: the facts block and tiles leave the card; the Results and How it ran lists become tabs.
  3. Patterns › stat tiles: the run page shows its figures as an unboxed row.
  4. Card head: "beside the stage bar the detail names only the step" grows to step, pass and time in step.
