What lies over a page (a menu, a picker, a popover, a panel, an editor, a Dialog) closes on a click outside and on Esc through one library call, dismissOnOutside; see docs/stillhet/panels.md. The menus below are drawn open, so they do not close here.

A new event in Pulse may play a chime: soundFor decides (the choice, a burst, away hours), playChime plays two soft notes from Web Audio, with no audio file.
