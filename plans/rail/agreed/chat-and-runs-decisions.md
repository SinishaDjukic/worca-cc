# Agreed · Rail for Chat and Runs: the first decisions (Wave 2)

Decided 5 Nov 2026. Wave 2 redesigns the daily loop toward the chat-first Worca (Wave 6). The rest of Wave 2 is still being
shaped (`docs/rail-shaping`, `plans/rail/shaping/chat-and-runs.md`); these four choices hold.

| # | Choice | Decided | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | Chat home | **The composer on top, then one list of conversations**: Needs you first, then Running, then Recent. The action strip, the five tabs and "More you can do" go; their actions move to the composer's +, Workflows to its own page. | Chat is the way in; one list with the waiting ones first is Pulse's Now for conversations. |
| C2 | A build in a conversation | **The compact build card**: title, place line, status line with its one action and ⋯, the stepper; "How it ran" opens the rest. | The conversation stays readable; the card says what matters and opens to everything else. |
| C3 | Runs | **An operations view**: every build across conversations, schedules and features, with the list's sentence, grouping, the end column and bulk actions. | Chat is the way in; Runs is where you find, sort and act on many. |
| C4 | Where a build opens | **A full page**, the trail Chat › the conversation › the build. | One navigation model: a thing opens as a page, the trail is the one way back. |

Not decided yet: C5, what a build with no conversation belongs to (schedules, re-runs, PR fix runs); it is shaped with
Wave 6, and Wave 2 keeps such builds in Runs only.
