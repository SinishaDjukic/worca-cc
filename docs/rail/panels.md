# Panels, side columns, conversations

| Rule | What | Why | Held by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `panel.over` | A panel opens over any page under its button; it has no page of its own | It never changes where you are | pulse-sweep |
| `panel.side-column` | A side column pushes above 1080px, covers to 761px, takes the window below; one at a time | Same as the app's need, in Rail's motion | worca-sweep |
| `panel.dismiss` | What lies over a page closes on a click or tap outside and on Esc; a click inside never does; focus returns to what opened it | One habit, on every page | worca-sweep |
| `panel.terminal` | Show in terminal opens a TerminalPane under its command: the live output, one input line, Stop and Close. A shell that is gone reads "Shell closed" and opens nothing; one that ends while open adds "Shell closed" and turns its input off | The command's own place keeps its shell; Close never ends it | stillhet-compare |
| `panel.chat-composer` | The composer's sentence says what the next message runs with: the chat's project, its model (grouped under its engine) and that model's effort, and from Advanced its Sets (each set in play a tick; only a set that brings a server or a skill). Each is a SentencePicker; in a chat a pick changes the chat once Worca has it, on Chat home it rides the first message. A chat's worktrees sit in its head from Expert ("Worktrees · N"), each removed after its own question under the head; a followed task's ⋯ sends it a direction, in a field under its card | What a message runs with is said where it is written, and nothing changes before Worca has it | stillhet-compare |
| `panel.answer-refs` | A task, a chat or a PR that Worca names in its answer is a RefChip in its words: a task's leads to its card in its chat, a chat's to the chat, a PR's opens the PR in a new tab, each with its own title. A name Worca has not confirmed (a bare id that may be no run) stays plain words; nothing is guessed | What Worca names is one click away, and never a dead link | stillhet-compare |
| `panel.plan-folds` | A Shaper plan's detail stays folded, never more chrome on the PlanCard: each role's verdict is a row in Reviewed by (its findings fixed, disputed, open; the line counts the open ones); the plan's per-requirement coverage heads Read the plan, one row per requirement (covered by which step, or what is still to fix); Fix all is in its ⋯ when a shape check or a requirement fails and the plan can be revised; its open decisions are OptionLists under the card, each set to Worca's pick, carried by Approve and build and by Ask for changes. A feature's total cost and time since its first run are in its TaskCard's folded Figures | The card stays as simple as drawn; nothing dev shows is lost | stillhet-compare |
| `panel.ship-card` | A feature's ship is the Ship ChangeProposal: one row per agreed requirement with its mark in a SentencePicker (Met; Not met asks where it moved, Dropped asks why, in a line under the card), then how it ships (one PR or stacked PRs, Worca's suggestion first), Create PR beside Not now. Create PR is off, saying how many are left, until every requirement is marked | Nothing ships with a requirement nobody looked at | stillhet-compare |
| `panel.conversation` | A conversation holds the person right, Worca on the page, work as cards on 760px | Readable at any width | by hand |
| `panel.conversation-rail` | What Worca does sits on one rail: a mark, one quiet line, what it took. Commands are dev's: run one after another they are one group folded to its head (Ran 3 commands · 1 failed · 5.4s; while one runs its summary leads, Stop beside it; a failure opens it); each command is a row with no mark whose words say how it went (done quiet ink, failed quiet red with its exit code, running counting), and one fold opens the command with Copy, where it ran with Show in terminal, and its output with Copy (CommandRow, CommandGroup). Thinking is one row (ThinkingRow): Worca's orb (OrbMark, the app's sphere of dots, its W facing you as the sphere turns under it) turning, faster while it writes, and one line saying what it does now in plain words ("Thinking" only until it says) with a slow light passing through it, and its seconds, all updated in place; done, the same row reads "Thought for 8s" with what it did folded under it. Thinking and commands fold; a failure opens itself; Send becomes Stop | A long conversation stays readable | by hand |
| `panel.conversation-cards` | One filled button on the screen, and only a Warning has it (`rail.one-action`): a failed card's own action is filled, a Caution or Advisory card's is outlined. Every card's mark sits on the rail's column and its title, line and body on the text edge; a one-line card is a plain row. A danger is quiet red words. A choice inside a card is a SentencePicker, never a grey bar; a workflow's steps are rows. An action that needed a dialog asks in a line under its card, and Ask for changes is a field under the card it is about, so nothing leaves the chat. The way out is Not now. Every card in a conversation is Rail's, in one shape (CardHead: a kicker with its mark and one word, the title, one line, its facts, ⋯ at the head's right; then its body; then one primary action beside quiet ones; then, if it has one, its one fold as its foot: Show its stages, Customise, Read the plan, opening under the card). Its body has no frame of its own (never a box inside a box); a change card's mark is its kind's (a schedule's clock, a project's folder); the deciding button of a task that waits on you is the card's primary, so the newest such card holds the one fill; a stop or an abort is quiet red on every card and row. A card says one thing once: no second line restating its head, no chips or grey buttons in its body (a choice is a picker in its sentence, a rarer way is in ⋯), a change's details are its rows (name → what it becomes), and what it shows is true now: its stage is its state's, Asks you below only when a question waits below, and a step taken anywhere (its card, its open stages, Away) moves its head, the bar and Pulse together. A TaskCard's How it ran opens the card in place with its task page's own stage stream, parts and controls; waiting or scheduled it is one line (Start now, Cancel), opened in place on demand; a feature lists its slices; a command running counts its seconds and folds its output when done; a task named in what you said is a chip that leads to its card. Every flow dev has reaches its cards here, step by step (plans/rail/chat-cards.md › Every flow): a TaskProposal with every dev flow, in the prototype's shape (when, effort, the brief and its files, Shaper's perspectives and Start planning, Run as is or Shape it first, Customise with where it runs, branches, guardrails, labels, memory, asks you and the agents lane, Open in the composer); a TaskCard's actions act in place from run-state's table (a move, a confirm line under it, a dialog on its page); Shaper's clarify questions (Use defaults), its receipt, its plan (Shape check, perspectives, ⋯), its split, gates, feature controls and ship; a ChangeProposal with a kind's own choices (web access, a schedule's Move, a workspace's re-scan); a command's Stop, Copy and Show in terminal; your own written answer. a task Worca proposes is a TaskProposal (the running card's shape, the composer's own sentence and Customise, Start beside Not now; Start turns it into the running card in place; open decisions offer Shaper); any other change it proposes (a setting, a workflow, a schedule) is a ChangeProposal, before → after, Apply beside Not now, a receipt with Revert once applied; a one-line offer or warning (a PR to open, a stop that loses work, a project to switch to) is a TurnRow with its actions at its end; a change of ground is a TurnDivider; a question of the chat itself is answered under it, and several take Worca's picks at once; the Shaper's plan is a PlanCard (its version, Reviewed by N roles · M open folding to one row per role, Approve and build), its disagreements and routes are questions, its changes a ChangeProposal, its ship a PR offer; files are a FilesCard (pictures as tiles, files as rows, Download); what you said is a YouTurn, with the quote you answered and the files you attached | One family of cards: a person learns one shape | by hand |

| `panel.center` | Tasks and Schedules land on a center: PluginCards from the one store, so a card changes when its task or schedule does. | `panel.center-shelf` | Projects lands on a center too. A project is one card, its actions named when two tasks wait. **A panel** (Pulse, the profile menu): opens under its top-bar button over any page, never as a page of its own.
Its head holds Tabs with each count as a caption and ⋯ at the right; the body scrolls and the head stays. On a narrow
screen it is the window's width.

**A side column** (Ask worca, a Feature chat): a column beside the page, opened from its top-bar button. Above 1080px
it pushes the page; from 761 to 1080px it covers the page's right side; at 760px and below it takes the window. Its
width is dragged between 360 and 680px and kept. It opens with Motion's fade, the page narrowing with it, and closes
at once. One side column at a time.

**The Chats column** (reference for Wave 2; in Wave 2's Shaping it is gone, the Chat list lives in the one bar, `nav.one-bar`) is a side column on the page's other side, with its own width: beside the conversation on Chat,
by the same three tiers and limits; at 760px and below it takes the window until a chat opens, and the conversation's
list button brings it back.

**A conversation** (Chat, a side column): the person's messages on the right on `--field`, Worca's text on the page,
its work as cards on a 760px text column under the shared card head: a run card, a command group (folded to its head;
a failure opens itself), a graph in its well.

**What closes how.** A menu, a picker, a popover, a flyout, a panel (Pulse, the profile menu), search results and an editor
close on a click or tap outside and on Esc. A click closes every open layer it landed outside; Esc closes the top one only. A click inside never closes them. That click does nothing
else, unless it lands on a real control: another menu's button or another top-bar button closes the first and opens the
second, in one click. A Dialog closes on its scrim only when nothing was typed in it; with typed text it stays, since nothing
typed is lost, and Esc follows the same rule. The ConfirmStrip and a side column stay: they are not over the page. The focus
returns to the control that opened it. Every page calls `dismissOnOutside(doc, layer)` (`dismiss.mjs`), one registry per
document, and keeps no outside-click code of its own.
