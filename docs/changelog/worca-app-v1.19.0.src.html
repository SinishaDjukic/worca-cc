Design

Design pages, decks and videos with agents.

Need a landing page or a pitch deck, not code?
Now open Design and describe it. A lead agent plans the sections. Then one agent builds each section, and you watch them work side by side.

Shaper

Agree what to build before planning.

Ever approved a plan that solved the wrong problem?
Now Shaper agrees the requirements with you first. It takes three cards, and usually three clicks: pick who leads, agree the requirements, then approve a plan that covers them all.
