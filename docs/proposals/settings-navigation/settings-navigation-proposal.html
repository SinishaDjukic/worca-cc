Worca Settings Navigation
Proposal · Worca UI · Settings

One topic per page, with a summary in front

Each Settings tab is one page of stacked cards, and the items under it in the side list only scroll that page. General now holds 11 cards. Runs holds 14. This proposal turns each card into its own short page, puts a summary page in front of each section, and regroups the cards by what people come to do. It covers every card Settings has today.

What is wrong today

30cards across 9 Settings tabs, plus 5 tabs that are a single page
14cards on Runs, the longest page; General follows with 11
25 of 30cards sit on those two tabs
0pages that show the state of a whole section at a glance

The length is a symptom. The cause is that a tab is one page, so every new feature makes a page longer. Unrelated jobs share a page: Runs mixes budgets, the corporate proxy, pull requests and where agents execute. Read-only status cards sit between forms that each have their own Save button.

The proposal in six moves

A · Pages

Each card is a page

Appearance, Budget, Secret vault and the rest each render alone, at the address the search already uses (#settings/<section>/<card>). The side list keeps two levels and looks the same.

B · Summary first

A section opens on its overview

Clicking a section shows one tile per page with its current state in a line: "Vault · On, 3 secrets", "Budget · $20 per run". Most visits end there.

C · Grouping

Regroup by intent

General and Runs split into six sections. Two are new: Security, and Execution. No section holds more than seven pages.

D · Search

Search lands on a page

The ⌘K box already indexes every card with its alternative words. It opens the page instead of scrolling to it.

E · Saving

One save bar per page

A page with unsaved changes shows a bar at the bottom: what changed, Discard, Save. Status-only pages show none.

F · Phone

Drill in, drill out

Under 720 px the list becomes the first screen: section, then page, with Back. The overview tiles double as that list.

Try both, with every card

The sketch holds all 30 cards, the five single-page tabs and the Credentials card that appears with a credential broker, with their real titles, interface levels and Labs flags. Switch structure, click through the side list, and search in the proposed one. Card contents are drawn as placeholders: the cards themselves do not change.

A Advanced and E Expert are the interface level a card needs. Labs marks a card behind a Labs flag. The one-line states on the overview tiles are examples.

Options I would not take

OptionWhy not
Collapse the cards into an accordionHides the length without fixing it. An open card still pushes the rest away, and Runs would be 14 closed bars.
Tabs across the top of a sectionFourteen tabs do not fit, and a second navigation axis competes with the side list.
Keep the long page, add a sticky "on this page" listThat is what the side list already is. It makes scrolling faster, not shorter.
A dialog per cardWorks for one quick edit. Fails for cards with status, help text and several actions, such as the vault or the proxy.
Three levels in the side listKeeps Runs as one section, but deep trees are slow to scan. Two short sections read faster than one nested one.

How to build it without a big rewrite

Pages

Show one card per route. The cards and their modules stay as they are, and the card addresses the search uses already exist.

Overviews

Each card exports a one-line state. The Security card's rows already are that. The section route lists them as tiles.

Regroup

Move the cards into the new sections. Old addresses redirect. Update the search catalogue, docs/ui-levels.md and the help links that say "Settings › Runs › …".

Save bar and phone

Replace per-card Save with the page bar where a page has a form. Add the drill-in list under 720 px.

Steps one and two give most of the benefit and can ship without moving any card. Step three changes where people look for things, so it wants a changelog note and the redirects. Text elsewhere that names a location, such as "Set in Settings › Runs › Chat runs: source branch", has to follow.
