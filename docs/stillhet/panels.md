# Panels, side columns, conversations

| Rule | What | Why | Held by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `panel.over` | A panel (Pulse, the profile menu) opens over any page under its top-bar button and has no page of its own; its head holds Tabs with counts and ⋯, and its body scrolls under it | It never changes where you are | pulse-sweep |
| `panel.side-column` | A side column (Ask worca) pushes the page above 1080px, covers its right side to 761px and takes the window below; one at a time, its width dragged between 360 and 680px and kept | It keeps the page readable at every width | worca-sweep |
| `panel.dismiss` | What lies over a page (a menu, a picker, a popover, a panel, search results) closes on a click or tap outside and on Esc: a click closes every layer it landed outside, Esc the top one only, a click inside never does, and focus returns to what opened it. Pages call `dismissOnOutside` and keep no outside-click code | One habit, on every page | worca-sweep |
| `panel.dismiss-typed` | A Dialog closes on its backdrop or Esc only when nothing was typed in it; the ConfirmStrip and a side column are not over the page and stay | Nothing typed is lost | by hand |
| `panel.terminal` | Show in terminal opens a TerminalPane under its command: the live output, one input line, Stop and Close. A shell that is gone reads Shell closed and opens nothing | The command's own place keeps its shell; Close never ends it | stillhet-compare |
| `panel.conversation` | A conversation holds the person's messages on the right on `--field`, Worca's words on the page, and work as cards on a 760px column | Readable at any width | by hand |
| `panel.chat-composer` | The composer's line says what the next message runs with: the chat's project, its workflow, its model (grouped under its engine) with that model's effort, and from Advanced its Sets. In a chat a pick changes the chat once Worca has it; on Chat home it rides the first message | What a message runs with is said where it is written, and nothing changes before Worca has it | stillhet-compare |
| `panel.starters` | Chat home's ways to start are StarterCards in a grid of 3, 2 or 1 by width: an icon, a name and one plain line, the whole card one button, pinned workflows first and More last. A starter only arms the composer; More also holds the labelled forms (New task form…, New schedule form…) | The ways to begin are scanned as cards, and nothing is sent until you send | design-system-task-redesign |
| `panel.voice` | Voice is one button with a menu (Speak, Hands-free, Voice options); while it listens, the same button stops it | One place for talking, and the way to stop is where you started | by hand |
| `panel.prompt-check` | With Prompt check on, a task-like draft shows its kind and its grade as a Chip; it never holds Send, and Needs detail offers Improve, a RewriteSuggestion above the composer (Use this, Keep mine, Undo) | Advice on a prompt never blocks it | stillhet-compare |
| `panel.chat-worktrees` | From Expert a chat's worktrees sit in its head (Worktrees · N), each removed after its own question under the head | What a chat left on disk is seen and cleared where the chat is | stillhet-compare |
| `panel.answer-refs` | A task, a chat or a PR that Worca names in its answer is a RefChip in its words: a task's opens the task's page, a chat's the chat, a PR's the PR in a new tab. A name Worca has not confirmed stays plain words | What Worca names is one click away, and never a dead link | stillhet-compare |
| `panel.rendered-prose` | Worca's finished words are rendered prose: classic's markdown renderer and sanitiser, headings at body size, tables as the Stillhet Table, code as a plain monospace block, scroll boxes reachable by Tab; streaming words stay plain | A reply reads like classic Chat's, and never outranks the page | design-system-prose |
| `panel.turn-time` | Every turn has its time, shown on hover, focus and tap in the gap under the turn at its right edge, moving nothing: 14:32 today, then Yesterday 14:32, then Nov 3, 14:32, in local 24-hour time | When something was said is there when you look for it, and never in the way | design-system-turn-time |
| `panel.conversation-rail` | What Worca does sits on one rail: a mark, one quiet line, what it took; thinking and commands fold, and a failure opens itself | A long conversation stays readable | by hand |
| `panel.commands` | Commands run one after another are one CommandGroup folded to its head (Ran 3 commands · 1 failed · 5.4s); each CommandRow's words say how it went, and it opens to the command with Copy, where it ran with Show in terminal, and its output with Copy | Ten commands read as one line until one matters | by hand |
| `panel.thinking` | Thinking is one ThinkingRow updated in place: Worca's orb turning, one line saying what it does now, and its seconds; done, the same row reads Thought for 8s with what it did folded under it. While Worca replies, Send becomes Stop | You see that Worca works and on what, without a growing log | by hand |
| `panel.conversation-cards` | Every card in a conversation has one shape: a CardHead (a kicker with its mark and one word, the title, one line, its facts, ⋯ that floats), a body with no frame of its own, one primary action beside quiet ones, and at most one fold as its foot. Which card serves what is the table below | One family of cards: a person learns one shape | by hand |
| `panel.card-one-fill` | One filled button on the screen, and only a Warning has it (`stillhet.one-action`): a failed card's action is filled, a Caution or Advisory card's is outlined, and a stop or an abort is quiet red | Filled means it stopped | by hand |
| `panel.card-says-once` | A card says one thing once, and what it shows is true now: no second line restating its head, and a step taken anywhere (its card, its open stages, its page) moves its head, the bar and Pulse together | A card and the rest of Worca never disagree | by hand |
| `panel.card-asks-in-place` | An action that needs a word asks in a line under its card, Ask for changes is a field under the card it is about, and the way out is Not now; a question is answered on its own form under the card, and a message typed in the composer is never read as its answer | Nothing leaves the chat, and an answer never lands on the wrong question | by hand |
| `panel.every-flow` | Every flow classic has reaches its cards in Chat, step by step; the list of flows and their wiring is `plans/stillhet/spec/wiring.md` | Chat is the one place to start and change work, so it must hold every way | by hand |
| `panel.task-card` | A task in a conversation is a TaskCard that answers two questions, what is its state and what do I do now, in this order: the state word and title; the stage train, with one small icon at its right end that opens the detail panel; one message in Worca's words (what happened and what you can do); the main action; the vitals (time, cost, changes). Nothing on it opens in place; a knot opens the panel at its stage; its title opens the task's page | The card is read at a glance; what you investigate has its own place (`panel.detail-panel`) | design-system-task-redesign |
| `panel.detail-panel` | What you open to investigate a task lives in one side panel beside the chat, in tabs (Activity, Changes, Decisions, Checks): a log opens in it at the relevant spot with failing lines marked, a diff opens wide. It opens only on intent (a link in the card's message, a knot, a vital, the card's panel button) and never by itself; it closes with its control, Esc or the same trigger and stays open while you click elsewhere in the chat; it follows the task you point at and names it in its header. On a tablet it is a sheet, on a phone full screen | The chat stays readable while you dig, and nothing opens that you did not ask for | by hand |
| `panel.detail-remembers` | The panel remembers, per person, whether it is open, its last tab and its width; it has a widen control for logs and diffs, marks a tab quietly when something new arrives, and an address can open it at a spot | You find it as you left it | by hand |
| `panel.detail-where` | Where details open is a setting, Settings › Appearance › Task details: Side panel (the default) or Inside the card. It holds for every card in a chat whose details would open in place: a task, each task of a feature, and a schedule's tasks (with the side panel, a schedule's card opens its page) | One habit for every card, and the person who prefers the details in the flow keeps them there | by hand |
| `panel.status-once` | A task's status speaks from its card only; it is never repeated as a chat message | One fact, one place (`stillhet.one-place`) | by hand |
| `panel.task-card-asks` | A card that asks shows what it asks about (What the review found, with its lines and a link to read it on the task's page) and each open decision, on the folded card | You can answer from the card without opening anything | design-system-task-redesign |
| `panel.task-depth` | A task card opened shows what its page has, on its own stages: each stage's time, cycles and cost, its Log and Artifacts as folds (`thing.open-line`), and findings to confirm as a question under the stage that asks | The card answers what the page would, where you are; the page stays for the long view | design-system-components |
| `panel.proposal-keeps-picks` | A proposal is labelled rows in fixed places (Project, Workflow, Model, Starts, Then) plus one supporting line, and carries the composer's own picks; a value it changed shows what it was, struck through, and the word changed | Nothing you chose is switched silently, and you can judge what you are asked to accept | design-system-task-redesign |
| `panel.proposal-workflow` | A workflow proposal shows its project and its WorkflowStory, and offers Run once beside Save workflow | You see what a workflow does before you keep it | by hand |
| `panel.plan-folds` | A Shaper plan's detail stays folded on its PlanCard: each role's verdict is a row in Reviewed by, per-requirement coverage heads Read the plan, and its open decisions are OptionLists under the card, each set to Worca's pick | The card stays simple; nothing classic shows is lost | stillhet-compare |
| `panel.ship-card` | A feature's ship is one ChangeProposal: a row per agreed requirement with its mark (Met, Not met, Dropped, the last two asking why under the card), then how it ships; Create PR is off, saying how many are left, until every requirement is marked | Nothing ships with a requirement nobody looked at | stillhet-compare |
| `panel.question-forms` | A question whose answer is not a choice of words is a small form under its row: a number on a SliderPick, an order on a RankList, rows on PickRows; the card's one filled button answers and a quiet one takes Worca's pick | Every answer is given the same way, where the question is | design-system-components |
| `panel.timeline` | A chat's anchors are a short stack of thin bars at the upper left of the chat, the current one in ink; hovering or focusing the stack pops their names and times up with one short fade, and a click jumps there. Under 760px it is not drawn; Latest remains | You can move through a long chat without the anchors competing with what you read | design-system-task-redesign |
| `panel.place-head` | A thing's own page (a schedule, a project, a workflow, a plugin, a feature, a workspace) starts with a PlaceHead: its title, its facts on one line, then its state with one action and ⋯; it has no composer, and its head offers one bordered Ask about it in chat | Every page of a thing reads the same, and talking about it is one click away | design-system-components |
| `panel.card-opens` | On a place's page (Projects, Marketplace, Workflows, Tasks, Schedules) a card's title is a link and the whole card opens the thing's page; a card has one bordered action (from classic's linked card titles) | The card is the way in, and one button says what you can do without opening it | by hand |
| `panel.plugin-card` | A plugin is a PluginCard on the cards' rail, never a boxed tile: its brand as the mark (a plug when it has none), kind and version, name, one line on what it does, Worca's reason when suggested, one line of facts, and its one action at the foot; cards sit two to a row under a shelf named for what you want | A catalogue reads like the rest of Stillhet, and the reason you would want it comes first | design-system-components |
| `panel.plugin-manage` | An installed plugin's page leads with how it is doing (Health, Test connection, Enabled), then its settings, what it adds and where it comes from; a chat-channel plugin holds its channel there, and Settings › Chat links to the plugin, never the other way | A plugin is managed where you found it (`set.one-fact`) | by hand |
| `panel.project-tabs` | A project's page is tabs (Overview, Tasks, Memory, Sets, Actions, Automations, Readiness, Settings), drawn by Tabs in one row that scrolls sideways when narrow (from classic's project tabs) | A project holds too much for one long page, and each part has a name you can go to | by hand |
| `panel.center` | Tasks and Schedules land on a center that only inspects: cards from the one store under one PageFilters line, with Figures and Start one in Chat in its end slot; nothing there starts work (`stillhet.chat-first`) | A place you land on says what wants you, and you always know where work begins | design-system-places-redesign |
| `panel.center-card` | A center card's mark is its top task's and its first word its state in its level's colour; its one action fits its state (`cardAction`: Retry, Answer, Approve, Create PR, Merge, Fix checks, Resume, Run now, Stop) and acts on that thing; only Retry is filled | What wants you can be answered where you see it | design-system-components |
| `panel.center-confirm` | A card action that asks first (Merge, Fix checks) asks at the card: the ConfirmStrip takes the place of the card's buttons, with the thing's own facts in its task page's words; Esc cancels and focus returns | A confirm opens where you pressed, never somewhere above | by hand |
| `panel.center-holds-order` | Cards do not reorder after an action: the acted card stays and shows its new state, and the order settles on the next visit or a new Order pick | Nothing moves under the pointer | by hand |
| `panel.center-narrow` | A card's shortcut to its list is its own button (Only web-app), every number opens what it counts, and a project with nothing going on folds into one quiet line; with nothing waiting, Tasks says Nothing needs you | A count is a way in, and calm work takes one line | design-system-components |
| `panel.center-shelf` | From Advanced, Projects lands by workspace, each a CenterShelf: its name, Workspace map, its worst state word, cards for what needs you, and its quiet projects folded into one line; On their own is the last group. Simple has one card per project | You see which workspace wants you before which project | design-system-components |
| `panel.center-also` | A project in two workspaces is one card under the most urgent and a CenterAlso in the other (Also in Web shop · Answer there), counted once | A shared project never counts twice | design-system-components |
| `panel.project-word` | A project card's word is its state: Failed, Question, Needs approval, Folder missing, Out of sync, Schedule missed; a copy only behind is Your copy is older, in grey, and the bar shows the same mark | One word for a project wherever you look | design-system-components |
| `panel.run-outcomes` | A schedule's recent tasks are RunOutcomes: a square each, oldest first (`--ink-2` succeeded, a ! failed, ink-ringed running, amber-ringed missed), its tooltip naming them, one line beside | How it has gone, at a look | design-system-components |
| `panel.task-truth` | A started schedule is a task and counts in its project's tasks. A missed time never started: it is not a task, but it needs you, as a clock row in Needs you on Tasks and in Pulse | Each count counts one thing | worca-sweep |
| `panel.pulse-read` | Opening an update marks it and its siblings read for you, in every tab and after a reload; it stays in its list, read, until you clear it, and reading ends the icon's news. What clears a Now row is said in its tooltip | A row you opened never asks for you again, and a row that waits says how it stops waiting | stillhet-pulse-read-host-1 |
| `panel.pulse-action` | A Pulse Now row that needs you carries its one action as a bordered button, by the center card's rule (`cardAction`); an update whose task moved on elsewhere drops its action and reads Resolved | Pulse answers what Tasks would, with the same button | stillhet-pulse-read-host-2 |
| `panel.workflow-story` | A workflow is told in plain words as a WorkflowStory: a panel a step saying what happens to your task there, each loop in words under the steps it joins (found something? back to Build, at most twice), then how a task goes through it. Simple reads it alone; Advanced and Expert read it above the graph | You understand a workflow in seconds, before any graph | design-system-components |
| `panel.story-true` | A story's time, cost and counts show only once tasks have run the step: a workflow no task has run says No tasks yet, never $0.00; until a model has written the words, each step reads its own description | A story never guesses | design-system-components |
| `panel.graph-box` | A workflow's graph sits in a box that scrolls inside itself, with Zoom and Fit above it and always in view; at Fit its words are 12px or more, and a graph wider than the box says so | Nothing leaves the column, and a big drawing is still readable | design-system-places-redesign |
| `panel.expand` | Expand reads a drawing large: it fills its page and stops at the page's edges, never over the bars; Close or Esc returns | The whole graph is one click away without leaving the page | design-system-components |
| `panel.editor-bar` | A workflow's editor has one bar of bordered buttons in one row: Undo, Redo, Import, Export, Discard, Save (from classic's editor toolbar) | An editor's tools sit where editors keep them | design-system-places-redesign |
| `panel.workspace-map` | From Advanced, how your projects fit together is WorkspaceBoxes: a box per workspace with its count and only what is wrong or running; a box opens as a WorkspaceMap, its projects by who uses whom, each link an arrow with its word and what Worca is unsure of dashed | It holds at any size: boxes grow with workspaces, not projects | design-system-components |

## Which card for what

| In a conversation | Card |
|---|---|
| A task Worca proposes | TaskProposal: labelled rows, Start beside Not now; Start turns it into the TaskCard in place |
| A running or finished task | TaskCard; a feature lists its slices |
| Any other change Worca proposes (a setting, a workflow, a schedule) | ChangeProposal: before → after, Apply beside Not now, a receipt with Revert once applied |
| A one-line offer or warning (a PR to open, a stop that loses work) | TurnRow, its actions at its end |
| A change of ground | TurnDivider |
| Shaper's plan | PlanCard (`panel.plan-folds`); its ship is `panel.ship-card` |
| Files | FilesCard: pictures as tiles, files as rows, Download |
| What you said | YouTurn, with the quote you answered and your files as chips |
| A question | An OptionList or a question form under its card (`panel.question-forms`) |
| What Worca ran or thought | CommandGroup, CommandRow, ThinkingRow on the rail |
