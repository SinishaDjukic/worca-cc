# One rail: the philosophy

| Rule | What | Why | Held by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `stillhet.one-rail` | Everything aligns to one 20px rail and one text edge | Nothing floats, so a page is read straight down | ds-check |
| `stillhet.state-first` | The state word and colour come first, then what you can do; normal is off, only what needs you is lit (`look.alert-ladder`) | You see what is happening before anything else | run-page-sweep |
| `stillhet.one-place` | A fact appears once; everything else links to it | Two copies drift and make you compare | by hand |
| `stillhet.one-action` | Each state offers one main control; the rest sit behind ▾ and ⋯. The primary button belongs to a Warning (`look.alert-ladder`); a Caution or Advisory action is outlined. A page with no Warning keeps its own primary (the composer's send, Start, Save) | You never have to weigh buttons, and filled means it stopped | run-page-sweep |
| `stillhet.quiet` | Labels, banners and help appear only when there is something to say | Quiet pages make the exception visible | by hand |
| `stillhet.scan-first` | Facts lead, sentences support. What a person scans or chooses is a labelled value or a control in a fixed place; a sentence is at most one short supporting line in the secondary style, never the control surface. Nothing changes size or place when a value changes | A sentence of pickers has to be read every time and reflows with every pick; a labelled place is learned once | by hand |
| `stillhet.drafts` | What you make or change by hand (a workflow, an agent, a script, on its page or its canvas; a schedule's times and its Expert facts) is a draft until you save it: tasks keep running the saved one; a DraftBar on the thing says it is a draft, how many changes it holds, Discard and Save; leaving keeps the draft, and Drafts in the bar lists every one with its count; a thing never saved is a draft that nothing can run. A chat's proposal is a draft too: its card, until you apply it. A delete with Undo in its notice is a draft of a delete: nothing is sent until the notice ends (it times out, the next notice replaces it, or the page closes), and Undo sends nothing | You can try a change without breaking what runs, and come back to it | design-system-components |
| `stillhet.hand-to-chat` | Whatever a place sends to Chat arrives as a chip in the composer, its kind and its name, removable, and goes with the message. A workflow or project it picks is also set in the sentence; changing that there drops the chip. Pages call one handover, never pre-written text | What you chose is what Worca gets, the same way from every place | handover-check |
| `stillhet.chat-first` | Work starts in Chat and nowhere else: a task or a schedule is started and changed in the composer, from a starter or on its card, and Chat is the only place to type to Worca. Tasks and Schedules are views to inspect: they have no + and start nothing new, and a page hands itself to Chat with one **Ask about it in chat** (or **Change it in chat**) action. What exists stays answerable where you see it (Answer, Approve, Merge, Pause, Resume, Stop, Run now). Workflows, Projects and Marketplace keep their + for what a person builds by hand, in the words "by hand" | One place where work begins, so you always know where to go and what a page is for | by hand |
| `stillhet.work-and-talk` | A task is the work; a chat is where you talk about it. A thing's row, card title or search result opens the thing's own page, in every mode, and the bar keeps the place and its filters. The page names its chat and opens it (`thing.from-chat`); the chat's card names its task and opens its page | You always know whether you are looking at the work or talking about it | by hand |
| `stillhet.center` | A page outside Chat is first of all a center: what you land on for that thing, with the same or similar sidebar logic, adapted to the case (list.bar-model) | You always know where you are and what is there | by hand |
| `stillhet.still` | Nothing moves by itself; a click or repaint never scrolls the page | You never lose your place | run-page-sweep |

Every screen reads down one rail, and everything has one place on it. The table is the whole philosophy: each rule is said once, here.
